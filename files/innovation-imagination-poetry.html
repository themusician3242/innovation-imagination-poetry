Why Poetry?

Poetry is multifaceted and personal. It allows students to explore these themes in structured classroom environments, serving as a catalyst for imagination, creativity, and ultimately innovation.

Literature Review

The First Hand Experience of Poetry In The Classroom

Of 100 students who took COM1000...

53% had direct exposure to a Poetry-focused curriculum.
47% had an alternative (control) curriculum.

Results & Data

Imagination & Personality In The Classroom Increases Engagement:

40% agreed, and 57% strongly agreed that imaginative or creative classroom activities contributed to sustained attention and focus during learning.

50% of Desi's students agreed & 43% strongly agreed that poetry or creative expression influenced levels of engagement.

Letting Go of Academic Restraints Can Contribute To Academic Success:

56% of Desi's students reported poetry contributed to their academic experience.

56% of these responses included the terms "improved" and "supported" in relation to their academic skills or performance.

Here's What Desi's Students Had To Say

"Poetry or creative writing can also be helpful because it allows students to express emotions and perspectives that might not come out in regular academic writing."

"These activities help students express ideas in a more personal and engaging way, instead of only writing formal essays."

"By providing more ways to engage: visually, with spoken words, and in research, allows students to expand beyond their limitations of learning and engagement to express themselves."
